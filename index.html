About the league

Why GMS JV exists and what players get out of a season.

A competitive and developmental basketball experience for student-athletes in 6th and 7th grade.

Middle school basketball programs across Alachua County are strong, and there is plenty of young talent in North Central Florida. But many school teams are made up mostly of 8th graders, so 6th and 7th graders have had few chances to play real games. In the fall of 2025 the Gainesville basketball community came together to fill that gap and formed the Gainesville Middle School (GMS) JV Basketball League. Players competed at the Alachua County Sports & Events Center.

Our focus

  • Fundamental skillsFootwork, shooting form, spacing and defense come first.
  • Confidence and teamworkPlayers learn to trust each other and to share the ball.
  • Sportsmanship and characterWinning and losing the right way is part of the lesson.
  • Meaningful game experienceA real schedule with real games, including players who do not yet have a school-team spot.
  • Ready for varsityPlayers build the habits that prepare them for the middle school varsity level.
